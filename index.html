Orthogonal cuts through a 3-D neutron histogram, averaged over symmetry-equivalent voxels, with coverage-edge and outlier masking and a 3-D isosurface view. Files are read on your computer and never uploaded.

Drop a NeXus file here Mantid MDHistoWorkspace files written by SaveMD, or any NeXus/HDF5 file whose NXdata group holds a 3-D histogram. The volume is held in memory as float32 (about 260 MB for 401³ bins).

Slices. Each pixel is the equal-weight mean of the unique finite, unmasked voxels in the slab, pooled with all their symmetry equivalents. No smoothing or interpolation. Grey means no measured contribution. Color limits only affect the display. Click a plot to move the other two slices through that point.

Symmetry. Generators are closed into a group; every operation must map bin centers onto bin centers, and the viewer warns when the operations change the cell metric by more than 2%. Oblique axes use the reciprocal metric from the UB matrix.

Mask. Detector edges and weak normalization leave spuriously high values along coverage boundaries. Edge erosion removes measured voxels near unmeasured ones; the outlier cut removes voxels far above the median of their symmetry equivalents (robust 1.4826·MAD). The mask acts on the unsymmetrized data before averaging, and Show removed displays only what it takes out.

3D view. The volume is binned by an odd factor with blocks aligned to the origin, symmetrized and masked the same way, and drawn as a transparent isosurface with the current slices.
