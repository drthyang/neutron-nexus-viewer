Method

Slices

Each pixel is the equal-weight mean of the unique finite, unmasked voxels in the slab, pooled with all their symmetry equivalents. No smoothing or interpolation; grey means no measured contribution. Click a plot to move the other two slices through that point.

Symmetry

Generators are closed into a group; every operation must map bin centers onto bin centers. The viewer warns when the operations change the cell metric by more than 2%. Oblique axes use the reciprocal metric from the UB matrix.

Mask

Detector edges leave spuriously high values along coverage boundaries. Edge erosion removes measured voxels near unmeasured ones; the outlier cut removes voxels far above the median of their equivalents (1.4826·MAD). It acts before averaging.

3D view

The volume is binned by an odd factor with blocks aligned to the origin, symmetrized and masked the same way, and drawn as a transparent isosurface with the current slices as planes.
