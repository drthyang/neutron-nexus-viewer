NeXus slice viewer

Reciprocal-space slices of 3-D neutron histograms, computed in your browser

Drop a NeXus file here

Opens Mantid MDHistoWorkspace files written by SaveMD, and other NeXus/HDF5 files whose NXdata group holds a 3-D histogram.

The file is read on your computer and never uploaded. It is held in memory as float32 (about 260 MB for 401³ bins).

Group operations

Each slice is the equal-weight mean of the unique finite, unmasked voxels in the selected slab, pooled with all their symmetry equivalents when a symmetry is chosen. Operations are closed into a group and must map bin centers onto bin centers, so no smoothing or interpolation is involved. Grey means no measured contribution. Color limits only affect the display.

The 3-D view bins the volume by an odd factor (blocks aligned to the origin), applies the same symmetry, and draws a transparent isosurface of the block means together with the current slices.

Click a plot to move the other two slices through that point. Oblique axes use the reciprocal metric of the unit cell stored in the file; Nominal snaps direct-cell angles within 1° of 60°, 90° or 120°.
